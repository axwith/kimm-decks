# Researcher Software Before and After Illustration

## Visual Concept
Create a wordless two-part editorial illustration contrasting dependence on a generic manufacturer program with a researcher using a purpose-built program informed by an equipment manual and AI assistance. Both sides show the same short-haired researcher in a plain lab coat seated at an identical compact research bench. A generic laboratory measurement unit and its cable appear in both scenes. All interfaces and equipment are fictional, unbranded illustrations, never realistic screenshots.

## Composition
Use a wide 16:9 canvas divided visually into two equal scenes with open white space between them. On the left, the researcher leans forward toward a rigid, crowded desktop monitor full of many tiny abstract controls. A closed manual rests beside the equipment; the posture conveys inconvenience, not exaggerated misery. On the right, the researcher sits upright, calmly using a simpler monitor with three large functional controls and one abstract trace. An open illustrated manual and a small conversational sparkle motif feed into this tailored program through a subtle red connector. Use equal scales and matching baseline so the contrast is immediate. Include one small right-pointing arrow in the center gap.

## Artistic Style
Match the supplied reference image's flat editorial shapes, charcoal and gray structure, red accents, pure white background, crisp edges, and restrained visual density. People should have believable proportions and minimal facial details. Maintain the reference's neutral flat lighting with no photorealistic shadows.

## Details & Elements
Depict an ordinary bench instrument with a screen, two round sockets, and one cable. Avoid hazards, industrial machinery, detailed circuit schematics, or domain-specific vibration equipment. Left program appears inflexible through many equal-sized controls; right program feels tailored through fewer larger controls, not through a magic autonomous machine.

## Text Integration
Absolutely no words, letters, numbers, logos, labels, or pseudo-writing. Screen content is pictorial only.

## Technical Specifications
Widescreen 16:9 horizontal composition. High quality, crisp rendering. PNG, raw output with no post-processing, cropping, or resizing.
