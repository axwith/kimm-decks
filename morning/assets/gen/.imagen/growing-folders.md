# Research Folders Accumulating Over Time Illustration

## Visual Concept
Create a wordless editorial illustration of research materials accumulating into an organized, increasingly useful collection. Show exactly three separate groups across one wide canvas. The first group is a single open folder containing a few document sheets. The second group contains several fuller folders with organized papers. The third group is a rich but tidy mini-library of folders, documents, and bound notebooks. This is a conceptual illustration of growing collections, not a quantitative chart or claim about productivity.

## Composition
Arrange the three groups from left to right on a shared baseline with equal horizontal spacing. The first has a sparse silhouette, the second a medium silhouette, and the third a visibly fuller silhouette. All three groups use the same physical folder scale; growth is shown by adding organized items, not by enlarging a single folder. Keep ample pure white space around each group so their boundaries remain unmistakable. Use no separating panels, arrows, timeline, axis, numeric marker, or text. Make each group large and recognizable at presentation distance. Slight overlap within each group conveys an assembled collection, while clear gaps separate the groups.

## Artistic Style
Match the supplied reference image's flat editorial construction, crisp contours, red accents, charcoal structure, pale secondary gray, and pure white background. Retain its neutral even lighting and absence of gradients or realistic shadows. Use simple paper silhouettes with folded corners, occasional binder clips, and large blank tabs for tactile character.

## Details & Elements
Repeat a distinctive red-accented document in every group to imply continuity. In the final group, include one open folder displaying several neatly aligned documents rather than a messy paper explosion. Avoid floating decorative objects and unnecessary technology motifs. No hands or people are needed.

## Text Integration
No letters, numbers, captions, logos, labels, filenames, or pseudo-writing anywhere. All document faces and folder tabs remain wordless.

## Technical Specifications
Widescreen 16:9 horizontal composition. High quality, refined detail, crisp rendering. PNG. Preserve the raw generated output; no post-processing, crop, or resize.
