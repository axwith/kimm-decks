# Reusable Research Context Illustration

## Visual Concept
Create a wordless editorial illustration showing how useful research context survives across sessions. Four tangible scenes form one clockwise cycle: a conversation being captured as a document, a magnifying glass finding the relevant document, a person confirming the selected information with a check mark, and a fresh conversation receiving that verified document. The viewer should see continuing work rather than disconnected chats. This is a generic public concept, not an actual product interface.

## Composition
Use a wide 16:9 canvas. Arrange the four scenes around a low, horizontally stretched oval: recording upper left, searching upper right, confirmation lower right, retrieval into a new conversation lower left. Four short curved red arrows join neighboring scenes clockwise. Keep the center white and spacious. Individual motifs should be comparable in size, and the arrows should not cross objects. Each motif is a small scene combining documents and a tool, not an app-icon badge. No labels anywhere.

## Artistic Style
Match the supplied reference image's flat shapes, edge treatment, visual density, neutral illumination, and colors. Keep the pure white background and editorial character. Use the reference only for style, not for its scattered layout: this new illustration must communicate one coherent cycle.

## Details & Elements
Make one recognizable red-accented document recur throughout all four scenes. Use a plain notebook or document tray for recording, a magnifying glass for search, a large clear check mark for confirmation, and paired speech bubbles receiving the document for retrieval. No decorative human faces, robots, brains, or network-node patterns. No realistic screens or claims about software behavior.

## Text Integration
No letters, numerals, captions, brands, labels, or pseudo-writing. Simple pictograms and check marks are permitted.

## Technical Specifications
Widescreen 16:9 horizontal composition. High quality, crisp and refined. PNG. Deliver the raw generated image as-is without post-processing, cropping, or resizing.
