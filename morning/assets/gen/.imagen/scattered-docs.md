# Scattered Research Documents Illustration

## Visual Concept
Create an editorial illustration of research documents scattered across four separate communication tools. Depict an envelope inbox, a messenger conversation, a cloud drive folder, and an AI conversation window. These are abstract public concepts, not real software screenshots or branded interfaces. Each tool has its own little cluster of loose document sheets, visually disconnected from the others. The meaning should be immediately understood without a single word.

## Composition
Use a wide horizontal composition. Arrange the four tools across the middle in four equally readable zones, with alternating heights and a few document sheets drifting through the gaps. Keep a generous white perimeter, with the complete illustration comfortably inside the canvas. Use frontal two-dimensional views and a clear visual rhythm. No enclosing panel, no large background card. Make the tool motifs large enough to recognize at presentation distance.

## Artistic Style
Flat editorial cut-paper illustration with confident charcoal silhouettes, a few crisp outlines, and large white cutouts. Shape variation provides character without tiny decoration. Not glossy, not photographic, not a mock screenshot. Uniform neutral illumination; no directional light, gradients, or three-dimensional shadows.

## Color Palette
Pure white #FFFFFF background. Charcoal #27272A for structure, pale gray #E5E7EB for secondary sheets, and red #FC1C49 sparingly highlighting important documents and conversation elements. No other chromatic colors.

## Details & Elements
Envelope, speech bubbles, cloud-shaped folder detail, and a distinctive four-point sparkle inside the abstract AI window. Documents may contain large abstract rectangles only. Absolutely no letters, numbers, logos, labels, pseudo-writing, or watermarks.

## Technical Specifications
Widescreen 16:9 horizontal composition, landscape orientation. High quality, refined detail, crisp rendering. PNG. Deliver the raw generated image as-is; no post-processing, crop, or resize.
